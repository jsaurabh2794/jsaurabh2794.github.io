01.About me

I'm a full stack engineer based in Bengaluru, India with 9 years of experience building scalable web applications and backend services. My foundation is Java, Spring Boot and Node.js — REST APIs, microservices and the kind of boring reliability that keeps production quiet.

Over the last year I've moved deliberately into applied AI. I design and ship agentic workflows using LangChain agents and tools, build RAG pipelines that ground model output in real organisational data, and expose internal systems through MCP servers so AI clients can use them safely.

I've worked across EV charging infrastructure, financial services and enterprise procurement, which has taught me that the hard part is rarely the framework — it's understanding the domain well enough to model it correctly.
